How it works

Three steps. No setup.

  1. 1

    Add your project

    Pick a folder on your Mac. helloagents finds Claude Code on its own.

  2. 2

    Describe the task

    Write what you want in plain words, the way you'd ask a teammate.

  3. 3

    Review and merge

    See what changed, line by line. Keep it, or throw it away.

Why it's different

You stay in charge of your code.

  • Your files stay safe

    Every task gets its own copy of the project. Nothing touches your work until you say so.

  • Nothing is hidden

    Watch each step as it happens: what the agent read, what it changed, which tests passed.

  • Free to use

    It runs on your Mac with the Claude plan you already pay for. No extra subscription.
