How it works

Three steps. No setup.

  1. 1

    Add your project

    Pick a folder. helloagents finds the coding agents on your Mac and reads how to install and test your project.

  2. 2

    Describe the task

    Pick the agent and model, then write what you want, the way you'd ask a teammate.

  3. 3

    Review and ship

    See every change, line by line. Commit, push, open a pull request, or merge, in one click.

Why it's different

You stay in charge of your code.

Tests decide “done”

Your project's own checks run after every change. If they fail, the agent gets the failure and tries again.

Your files stay safe

Every task gets its own branch and folder. Nothing touches your work until you merge it.

Ship in one click

Commit, push, open a pull request written from the run's summary, or merge. Open it in Zed, Cursor or VS Code.

Many projects at once

Runs grouped by what needs you, what's working and what's done, across every project, with a notification when one finishes.

Nothing is hidden

Watch each step live. Traces show where the time and tokens went, and Errors collects every failure in one place.

Your agent, your plan

Claude Code runs on your Claude plan today; Codex on your ChatGPT plan is next. No API key, no extra subscription.
